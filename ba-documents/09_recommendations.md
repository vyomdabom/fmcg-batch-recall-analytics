Candidate areas to evaluate (do NOT just copy these — test them against your findings):

- Data capture standards + validation at goods-in
- Centralised batch register with enforced links (the model you built)
- FEFO picking rule supported by an expiry-priority view
- Supplier document register with expiry review cycle
- Complaint intake form requiring product/batch fields
- Weekly high-risk batch review meeting
- Management dashboard adoption + refresh cadence
- Longer-term: barcode scanning, lightweight inventory system evaluation
