## US-01 — Expiry visibility

As an **Operations Manager**, I want to see which batches are expiring soon so that I can reduce stock write-offs and prevent expired products from being dispatched.

## US-02 — Document compliance

As a **Quality & Compliance Coordinator**, I want to identify batches with missing or expired supplier documents so that I can follow up with suppliers before products are sold.

## US-03 — Recall contact list

As a **Sales Team member**, I want to know which customers received an affected batch so that I can contact them quickly during a product issue.

## US-04 — Recall exposure value

As a **General Manager**, I want to see the recall exposure value for a selected batch so that I can understand the financial and operational risk to the business.
