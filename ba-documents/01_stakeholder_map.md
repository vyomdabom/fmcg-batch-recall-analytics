# Stakeholder Map — FreshRoute Foods Ltd

> Starter content. Extend with an influence/interest grid and engagement approach — that part is yours to complete.

## 1. Dana Whitford — Operations Manager (project sponsor)

- **Role:** Owns warehouse operations, stock control, dispatch, and the batch register. Your direct manager.
- **Cares about:** Stock accuracy, dispatch speed, write-off reduction, being able to answer "where did that batch go?" in minutes.
- **Pain points:** Fire-fighting expiry issues, reconciling conflicting spreadsheets, the 6.5-hour trace incident.
- **Likely questions:** "What's expiring this month?" "How much stock value is at risk right now?" "How long would a recall take with your solution?"
- **Needs from the dashboard:** Expiry pipeline (30/60/90 days), high-risk batch list, stock value at risk, warehouse locations of risky stock.

## 2. Priya Sharma — Quality & Compliance Coordinator

- **Role:** Manages supplier certifications, quality checks, complaint investigations, and recall procedures; interface with MPI requirements.
- **Cares about:** Complete supplier documentation, complaint-to-batch traceability, audit readiness, recall speed.
- **Pain points:** Certificates buried in email, no alerts when documents expire, complaints logged without batch numbers, manual recall traces.
- **Likely questions:** "Which suppliers have expired or missing documents?" "Are complaints clustering on any batch or supplier?" "Can I produce a trace report for an auditor on demand?"
- **Needs from the dashboard:** Document status by supplier, complaint-batch linkage, recall traceability page, supplier quality scoring.

## 3. Warehouse Team — led by Tom Aiono (Warehouse Lead)

- **Role:** Receive stock, record batch/expiry at goods-in, pick and dispatch orders, do stocktakes.
- **Cares about:** Simple, fast processes; not being blamed for data errors caused by clunky tools.
- **Pain points:** Manual keying of batch numbers under time pressure, no validation in the spreadsheet, unclear FEFO (first-expired-first-out) picking guidance.
- **Likely questions:** "Will this mean more typing for us?" "How do I know which batch to pick first?"
- **Needs:** Clear data-entry standards, a pick-priority view (which batch/location to pick first), fewer duplicate records to untangle.

## 4. Mel Carter — Sales & Customer Support Lead

- **Role:** Takes orders (email/phone), maintains the sales order log, fields complaints, communicates with customers during product issues.
- **Cares about:** Fast, confident answers to customers; protecting key accounts.
- **Pain points:** Can't tell a customer which batch they received; complaint follow-ups depend on chasing other teams; duplicate customer records with inconsistent regions.
- **Likely questions:** "Which of my customers got the affected batch?" "What do I tell them and when?"
- **Needs from the dashboard:** Customer-level recall exposure, complaint status tracking, resolution time visibility.

## 5. Grant Hollis — General Manager

- **Role:** Overall P&L accountability; reports to the owners; approves investment in systems and process change.
- **Cares about:** Financial exposure, brand/customer relationships, compliance risk, cost-effective fixes.
- **Pain points:** No at-a-glance view of risk; finds out about problems by email after the fact.
- **Likely questions:** "What's our total exposure if we recall batch X?" "Which suppliers should we review or drop?" "What will fixing this cost vs the risk of not fixing it?"
- **Needs from the dashboard:** Executive overview KPIs, recall exposure value, supplier league table, trend direction.

## 6. Suppliers (external)

- **Role:** ~14 manufacturers/importers supplying product with batch documentation, certificates, and quality declarations.
- **Cares about:** Keeping FreshRoute as a customer; not being unfairly blamed for issues.
- **Pain points:** Chased ad-hoc for documents; inconsistent expectations on what to provide.
- **Relevance to project:** Their quality history and document compliance is a key analysis dimension; they don't use the dashboard but are affected by supplier review decisions it drives.

## 7. Retail customers (external)

- **Role:** ~70 supermarkets, dairies, cafes, restaurants, and small retailers.
- **Cares about:** Safe product, accurate shelf life on delivery, fast honest communication during issues.
- **Pain points:** Short-dated deliveries; slow answers when they raise a complaint.
- **Relevance to project:** Recall traceability exists to protect them; complaint data comes from them.
