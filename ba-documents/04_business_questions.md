# Business Questions

> Your analysis, SQL, and dashboard must answer these. Do not answer them here — answer them in the final insights report with evidence.

## Expiry & stock risk

1. Which batches are expiring within 30, 60, and 90 days?
2. What is the total stock value at risk (quantity remaining × unit cost) per expiry window?
3. Which products and storage types (chilled/frozen/ambient) have the most near-expiry stock?
4. Which warehouse locations hold risky stock?

## Recall & traceability

5. Which customers received a selected affected batch, and in what quantities?
6. Which regions are most exposed during a recall of that batch?
7. How many customers would be affected, and what is the total recall exposure value (sold value + remaining stock value)?
8. Are any complaints already linked to the affected batch?

## Supplier quality

9. Which suppliers have the most quality issues (failed checks, complaints, recall risks)?
10. Which batches have missing or expired supplier documents, and from which suppliers?
11. Which suppliers should management prioritise for review, and on what evidence?

## Complaints

12. Which products receive the most customer complaints, and of what type/severity?
13. Is complaint volume trending up or down, and is any batch or supplier over-represented?
14. What is the average complaint resolution time, and how many complaints are open?

## Data quality & process (BA questions)

15. What share of orders and complaints are missing batch identifiers — and what does that mean for traceability?
16. What process improvements would most reduce risk, and in what priority order?
